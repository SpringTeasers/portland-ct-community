Welcome to Portland, Connecticut

A small New England town on the Connecticut River — “Come on Over!”

Portland is a residential town in Middlesex County, Connecticut, set on the east bank of the Connecticut River across the Arrigoni Bridge from Middletown. Known for the brownstone quarries that shaped its early growth, Portland combines a quiet village character with modern town services, public schools, a public library, and a range of parks and recreational facilities.

About Portland

A quiet village on the river

Portland is a town in Middlesex County, Connecticut, located on the east bank of the Connecticut River, across the Arrigoni Bridge from Middletown. Incorporated in 1841, it is a residential community with active businesses and a dedicated industrial park.

To its residents, Portland is one of the best-kept secrets in New England — a village centered on families, friendships, and old-time traditions, with nationally acclaimed schools, active churches, responsive public works, full-time police services, and a modern library.
