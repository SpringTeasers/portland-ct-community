Utilities

Municipal water (supply sources: Metropolitan District Commission and a municipal well) serves approximately 3,000 users through 40 miles of water mains, primarily within the downtown area, Brownstone Industrial Park, and the most densely developed areas of Portland. Water and sewer billing questions: (860) 342-6735.

Sanitary sewer serves approximately 1,500 users within the downtown, industrial park, and adjacent areas.

Natural gas is available in limited areas, near Portland schools and the Glastonbury town line. Contact CNG to determine if natural gas is available on your street.

Transportation & Transit

Primary transportation is provided by state and local highways. The town owns a handicapped-accessible van for special programs. Freight delivery is available by rail into the industrial park area, and the Connecticut River is also available for transportation.

Bus service

Bus service is operated by the Middletown Transit District. It runs five days a week, connecting Portland and East Hampton to Middletown. The American Red Cross Dial-a-Ride service for the elderly and handicapped is also available five days a week.

Airports

Bradley International Airport is 27 miles north (about 40 minutes) off Interstate 91 in Windsor Locks. Buses to New York and Boston are available in Middletown.

Highways

  • Route 66 runs east–west through the southern portion of town.
  • Route 17 overlaps Route 66 for 2.42 miles and then runs north toward Glastonbury.
  • Route 17A consists of Main Street, running from the Arrigoni Bridge to where it meets Route 17 at the “Four Corners.”
  • Route 9 in Middletown connects to I-91 (Hartford/Massachusetts to the north, New Haven to the south) and to I-84.

Transit operator name not linked. The town’s pages give two variants — “Middletown Transit District” and “Middletown Area Transit” — and no verified website, so no transit authority link is published here.

Source: Town of Portland, Transportation and Utilities — portlandct.org/transportation-and-utilities
